This guide synthesizes community knowledge. The original sources below deserve the credit — consult them directly for the deepest dives.

## Primary references

- **Simplify3D Print Quality Troubleshooting Guide** — the classic symptom-by-symptom reference with real-world photos. Covers 25+ print defects with causes and fixes. <https://www.simplify3d.com/resources/print-quality-troubleshooting/>
- **Teaching Tech 3D Printer Calibration** — step-by-step calibration with a custom G-code generator (PID, e-steps, flow, temperature, retraction, linear advance). <https://teachingtechyt.github.io/calibration.html>
- **r/3Dprinting wiki** — community-maintained troubleshooting and buying advice. <https://www.reddit.com/r/3Dprinting/wiki/>
- **MatterHackers 3D Printer Troubleshooting Guide** — another thorough visual guide. <https://www.matterhackers.com/articles/3d-printer-troubleshooting-guide>

## Sovol SV01 specific

- **Sovol3D official site & downloads** — firmware, manuals, and slicer profiles for the SV01. <https://sovol3d.com>
- **Sovol user community (Facebook group)** — active owners sharing profiles, mods, and fixes; the best place for SV01-specific profile downloads.
- **CNX-Software SV01 review** — detailed review with real-world retraction findings (1.75 mm @ 10 mm/s on the stock hotend). <https://www.cnx-software.com/2019/09/25/sovol-sv01-review-a-3d-printer-that-prints-like-a-boss/>
- **Tom's Hardware SV01 Pro review** — notes on the Pro variant (touchscreen, ABL) and build-plate observations. <https://www.tomshardware.com/reviews/sovol-sv01-pro>
- **MakeWithTech forum: bed adhesion on the SV01** — glass-bed heat-soak discussion. <https://forum.makewithtech.com/t/bed-adhesion-problems-on-new-sovol-sv-01/2600>

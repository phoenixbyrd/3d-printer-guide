## What it looks like

Audible pops, hisses, or crackles from the nozzle while printing. The extruded plastic looks rough, frosty, or foamy instead of smooth and glossy. Stringing that won't tune away, weak layer bonds, and PLA that snaps when bent are all the same root cause.

## Causes

1. **Filament absorbed moisture from the air.** PLA does it in weeks, PETG and TPU in days. A spool left out is a wet spool.
2. **Printing too hot** makes the moisture boil more violently — but the moisture is still the problem.

## Fixes

1. **Dry the spool.** Filament dryer or food dehydrator at 50–60°C for 4–6 hours (stay under ~65°C for PLA or it softens and fuses). You can often print straight from the dryer.
2. **Store properly going forward:** sealed bags/bins with desiccant; toss a hygrometer in — recharge desiccant when it reads over ~30% RH.
3. **For humid shops or PETG/TPU regulars:** build a dry box you print from (sealed tub + desiccant + PTFE outlet tube).
4. Severely wet or old PLA can become permanently brittle — if it still snaps after drying, the spool is done.

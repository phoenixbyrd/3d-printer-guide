The formula behind it

  • Filament: grams used × (spool price ÷ spool grams), plus 10–15% for failed prints, brims, and purge.
  • Electricity: printer kW × hours × your $/kWh. On an SV01 this is usually the smallest line item — cents, not dollars.
  • Labor: slicing, support removal, cleanup, photos, listing, and meeting the buyer. This is usually the biggest cost people forget.

The 2–3× rule

Your costs set the floor; the market sets the ceiling. Pricing at 2–3× true cost keeps you profitable after the occasional failed print and the fees life throws at you. Before listing, check what similar prints actually sell for — if the going price can't clear 2× your cost, that print isn't worth selling.

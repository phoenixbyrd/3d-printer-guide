💡 Your costs set the floor; the market sets the ceiling. Check what similar prints sell for before listing — if the going price can't clear 2× your cost, that print isn't worth selling.

The formula behind it

  • Filament: grams used × (spool price ÷ spool grams), plus a waste allowance for failed prints, brims, and purge.
  • Electricity: printer kW × hours × your $/kWh. On an SV01 this is usually the smallest line item — cents, not dollars.
  • Labor: slicing, support removal, cleanup, photos, listing, and meeting the buyer. This is usually the biggest cost people forget.

The 2–3× rule

Pricing at 2–3× true cost keeps you profitable after the occasional failed print and the fees life throws at you. Tap the multiplier up top to switch between them — 2.5× is a solid default for Marketplace.
